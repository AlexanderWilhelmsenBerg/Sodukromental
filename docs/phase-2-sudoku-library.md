# Phase 2 · Sodukromental’s offline Sudoku player

**Player outcome.** Choose a Logic Masters Deutschland puzzle, save it once, then read the setter’s rules, solve and resume in Sodukromental’s own Flutter player, even after restarting the phone in airplane mode. **Play** and **Resume** always mean this player. SudokuPad supplies the reference for how solving feels. SudokuPad and F-Puzzles share links supply puzzle data during acquisition. The Phase 2 requirements below apply to the next debug build.

## Identifying Sven’s player

The product is **Sven Neumann’s SudokuPad**. Authors link puzzles to its web player, Cracking the Cryptic uses it, and Sven also publishes a separate Android companion. A setter explicitly labels the link on *All the Same and Never 4*. Use the documented and observable **web solving workflow** as the behavioral reference for our native Flutter player. Keep Sodukromental’s Library, author attribution, visual system and protected local progress. F-Puzzles is a separate creation/player format that SudokuPad can also import. [Developer’s site](https://svencodes.com/) · [Web player and controls](https://sudokupad.app/) · [Setter’s example](https://logic-masters.de/Raetselportal/Raetsel/zeigen.php?chlang=en&id=000HKK) · [Separate Android listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.svencodes.sudokupad)

### What the puzzle details screen should say

| Action | What the player gets |
| --- | --- |
| **Save for offline** | Fetch the setter’s chosen edition and its necessary assets. On a first online visit, **Download and play** may run this same operation and open the native board once installation succeeds. |
| **Play offline** / **Resume puzzle** | Open the verified local board or its saved attempt in Sodukromental. Bundled boards can open immediately on a fresh offline install. |
| **View saved diagram** | Read the locally saved author diagram and rules. If play is pending, say which feature the board needs. |
| **Read setter’s page** | Visit the Logic Masters entry for author credit, comments and the portal’s solution-code workflow. |
| **Watch CtC solve** | Open the verified YouTube video. Show a different-grid advisory for a known presentation change. The badge, filter, newest-featured-first sorting and video identity work from stored metadata. |

Keep **Automatically download Logic Masters puzzles** off by default. Every catalog entry stays discoverable regardless of download status. A verified package awaiting native support stays saved with a specific explanation, such as “Puzzle saved. This edition needs linked grids.” A complete local diagram has its own count under **Saved**. A listing, a player’s HTML shell or a YouTube page counts only as metadata. If first acquisition fails, show **Retry download** and its reason. Offline with only metadata, say “Connect to download this puzzle.”

The setter-page and video buttons are explicit reference actions. Import, edition selection, play, resume and support recovery stay inside the app. Neither a browser handoff, a deep link to another solver nor an embedded web player forms part of those flows.

## Selecting and storing the correct board

1. **Collect candidates.** Keep the Logic Masters ID and canonical setter page. Extract author-body player candidates with anchor text, paragraph context, order and source language. Include a link wrapping the puzzle picture. Preserve secondary editions, the portal diagram, video evidence and portal links separately. Candidates from a cited earlier puzzle, series index or explanatory example have negative evidence. A first-listed anchor or a shared author is insufficient.
2. **Resolve the player link.** Direct recognized inline `scl`/`ctc` and `fpuz`/`fpuzzles` data can decode without another request. SudokuPad’s official loader also supports `scf` and extensions such as packs. Recognize these and report unsupported contents unless an implemented decoder passes fidelity tests. Resolve an approved short ID or namespaced slug such as `blobz/azul` through `https://sudokupad.app/api/puzzle/<id>`, encoding each segment and preserving the slash. Handle `/sudoku/` on `sudokupad.app` and `app.crackingthecryptic.com`, and `?puzzleid=` on the web player. The companion host `sudokupad.svencodes.com/puzzle/<id-or-package>` has its own route. Do not strip `/puzzle/` from the primary host, where the same path can serve a generic HTML shell. F-Puzzles `?load=` carries encoded data. Its `?id=<slug>` adapter resolves `https://tinyurl.com/<slug>` through the same bounded redirect policy. Preserve literal `+`, `/` and padding inside encoded payloads. Separate presentation settings from the lookup key. Map supported presentation settings into the local definition and include material settings in the fidelity decision. Follow known author aliases such as TinyURL through a bounded chain (default five hops), require HTTPS and recognized alias/player hosts and route forms at each hop. Reject embedded credentials, nonstandard ports, loops and unexpected destinations from automatic resolution. Keep the original share URL as import provenance and a retry key. A direct inline payload in the redirect location can be extracted before requesting player HTML. [SudokuPad loader](https://sudokupad.app/puzzleloader.js?v=0.616.0) · [Compact decoder](https://sudokupad.app/puzzlezipper.js?v=0.616.0) · [F-Puzzles URL handling](https://www.f-puzzles.com/Main.js?v=1.11.3) · [Namespaced example](https://sudokupad.app/blobz/azul)
3. **Establish identity.** Prefer the setter’s explicit current “play/solve” or title-labelled target corroborated by decoded title, author, stable key or an independently verified identical package. Preserve aliases and original titles for comparison. Allow localized, missing or deliberately different titles when other exact evidence exists. Reject a different puzzle explicitly identified in the paragraph or payload. Deduplicate equivalent keys/packages. Do not merge merely by title. If there are distinct author-sanctioned editions, offer a labelled in-app edition picker. Each selection imports that edition into the native player. If ambiguity remains, show candidate choices with source context and leave automatic downloads unresolved. A verified CtC player can corroborate the exact author grid. A video, a title-only match or a modified broadcast grid cannot silently replace it.
4. **Validate bytes and capabilities.** A real SudokuPad API can return a valid `scl` string with a `text/html` MIME label. A player page can return actual HTML with the same label and status 200. Inspect bytes, decode a supported format as data, validate schema, playable cell mask, symbols, region and clue geometry, required assets and triggers. Recognize legacy compact objects and metadata stored in zero-cell cages. A successful decode alone grants no native play claim. Keep transport (`offline`, `retry`, `not found`), identity (`verified`, `multiple editions`, `ambiguous`), local capability and installed availability independently observable.
5. **Commit and repair.** Store portal revision, resolver version, source and resolved player URL, verified payload fingerprint, imported format, support report and local asset manifest. Store the verified source payload locally with the normalized board, original rule text and necessary clue assets. A retained source payload can be decoded again offline after an app upgrade adds a capability. Download and verify into staging, then atomically expose the chosen revision. A changed redirect behind an unchanged title or URL must be detectable. Re-evaluate old first-anchor/alias records once after upgrade even when the portal revision is unchanged. For old records without a fingerprint, compare the cached player URL and normalized stored board (givens, regions, clue layers and supported rules) to the newly verified identity. When equivalence cannot be established, keep the old attempt separate and explain the uncertainty. If an installed fingerprint is known to belong to another grid, quarantine its association with the intended title. Preserve its attempt separately with an explanation. Install the confirmed grid as a distinct attempt. Do not copy previous entries, notes or completion onto a different grid. Preserve verified good revisions during a timeout or storage failure. Keep manual and automatic jobs deduplicated by puzzle and revision/identity. Pause, retry and airplane-mode cold starts must retain honest availability.

## Native solver acceptance

A versioned local puzzle definition carries playable cells, symbols, givens, region membership, clue geometry and layer order, rules text, reveal triggers, presentation settings and asset references. Source decoders produce this definition. The Flutter board, input controller, checker and attempt store consume it. Opening an installed board reads only local data. Use the same path for a setter-provided F-Puzzles edition and its equivalent SudokuPad edition after checking their identity and authored differences.

### B4b · Faithful variant play

- **Render the setter’s board.** Preserve region outlines, killer cages and totals, ordered line paths including Renban and whispers, thermometer bulbs, arrows, dots, shading, text, and clues outside the grid. Keep layer order, alignment and contrast in light and dark themes. Pan to an outside clue and back without selecting an unintended cell. Match givens, geometry and visible clue layers against captured reference boards.
- **Use the puzzle’s alphabet and shape.** Build the keypad from the definition. Treat zero and negative values as playable symbols. Track an unfinished cell and a deliberate blank distinctly. Apply the declared regions and repeat policy. On *Balanced X-Sums*, place −3 and 0 in separate cells and check with no 3×3-box assumptions. On *The Magic Column*, allow repeats within rows and one empty cell per column. A solver can shade a deduced empty cell green. Completion must accept those blanks and preserve the setter’s diagonal, line-sum and magic-column instructions. Both fixtures require a playable native board.
- **Reveal fog locally.** Reproduce the imported initial mask, hidden clue layers, triggers and reveal footprint. For standard fog, test a correct corner entry and its clipped neighborhood. For a linked or explicit `cellvalue → foglight` rule, test exactly which remote cells open. A wrong entry leaves the mask alone. Match the authored undo behavior and restore the same mask after a cold start. Imported fog runs independently of campaign creatures. [Reference fog behavior](https://sudokupad.app/feature-fog.js?v=0.616.0)
- **State what Check knows.** Offer rule checks for implemented constraints, a final-grid comparison where a complete authorized answer applies, and **Manual check** for faithfully rendered puzzles with additional unevaluated rules. Identify the scope beside the result. An incomplete set of validators cannot certify a solve. A solution string alone cannot compensate for hidden or missing clues. Normalize numeric and string answers against the declared alphabet, including zero. Keep a manual finish distinguishable in the solve record.
- **Track remaining format coverage.** When a board needs an unimplemented capability, store the verified package, name the missing feature and offer its saved reference if available. Keep a fixture per capability, including overlapping grids, imported packs and custom dynamic effects. Promote each to native play after its rendering, interactions and offline resume pass. On an upgrade, re-evaluate saved packages locally and enable **Play offline** as soon as they qualify. #### Entering digits in fog

**Regular Sudoku accepts tentative moves.** In the standalone Library, select any playable covered cell by tap, drop, keyboard or the accessible row/column selector. Place a digit there, pencil candidates, replace the digit, erase it and use undo/redo. Draw and announce the player’s own entries above the fog. Accept a valid symbol even when it conflicts with a visible neighbor or differs from the solution, on clear and fog boards alike. A wrong guess persists until the player edits it. **Check** may explain a visible conflict. Entry itself gives no answer-key warning.

Store the authored clues, player marks and reveal state separately. A visible given stays fixed. For a covered given, allow the same tentative overlay as for a covered empty cell so focus, disabled controls and edit feedback cannot disclose its identity. The original clue stays intact. When its cell is revealed, display and lock the given, and keep the previous overlay in the undo history. Restore the matching input and visibility state when the puzzle’s undo policy covers it again.

Evaluate the setter’s trigger against the new move even if its cell began in fog, unless that trigger explicitly requires a lit cell. On a correct-only trigger a wrong entry stays visible to its author and earns no light. A qualifying move lights exactly the prescribed cells. On linked fog, a correct digit outside the trigger may earn nothing, and one of two prerequisites may be insufficient. Apply the same rules to pencil marks, replay and saved attempts: notes never count as a placed value. Use the edition’s declared reveal/undo behavior. An edition whose triggers cannot be reproduced stays pending native support. [Setter’s example of local and remote fog effects](https://logic-masters.de/Raetselportal/Raetsel/zeigen.php?chlang=en&id=000SP0)

Its linear picker filters out dark cells and the shared board hides their values and notes and rejects drops. Fixing the importer alone would leave these input paths unchanged. Enter 3, cold-resume with the 3 still visible above the fog, replace it with 7 and compare the exact reveal set. Undo to 3, redo to 7, then erase under the fixture’s reversible mask. Repeat with keyboard, touch/drop and TalkBack. Compare a covered given and a covered empty square before reveal. Test a wrong entry and an editable duplicate on a clear board too. Its turn deeper inside the cell, endpoint and next cell stay concealed. A line wholly behind the fog produces no cue. Preserve a setter’s stronger concealment when an entire clue layer is hidden.
