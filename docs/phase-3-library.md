# Phase 3 · Recover a playable Logic Masters puzzle

**Player journey.** Pick a Logic Masters Deutschland entry in **Sudoku**, download the setter's chosen edition while connected, then reopen it from **Saved** in airplane mode. Opening the setter's page or a Cracking the Cryptic video is an explicit reference action. Neither link starts or completes a game download. Automatic acquisition remains a preference and starts off.

## What the 0.2.0 report uncovered

The reported title was unspecified. We traced several independently reproducible ways an entry could look like “setter's page only.” In 0.2.0 the detail parser kept one ranked play link even when several were present. Some short resolver URLs were excluded. A saved reference hid the ordinary download and details refresh and could prevent a second attempt at the same revision. A successfully downloaded payload could also be rejected during decoding. Finally, some setters genuinely published only an illustration. The byline/profile, the canonical puzzle post, a playable share, a player HTML shell and a puzzle payload have different roles. ## Acquisition and recovery contract

1. Extract candidates from the authored puzzle content and keep their order, labels and context. Prefer a link for the current edition over a cited older puzzle. Try the next credible candidate for the same authored edition if the first is stale, unrelated, malformed or unsupported. If two distinct supported editions remain plausible, the Phase 3 target is a labeled choice with its own local identity rather than silently replacing one with the other. Only inspect recognized puzzle-specific intermediate pages and known redirect providers. Use bounded hops, a visited set and trusted HTTPS destinations. Author catalogs, video playlists, advertisements and the portal's navigation are ineligible.
2. Resolve to the payload and check its identity and native support before advertising **Play**. Save the chosen edition, source and resolved URLs and fingerprint. A well-formed HTML response alone cannot satisfy the download. A successful playable result takes priority over a reference fallback. Preserve the old verified local edition and its solve history through a failed refresh.
3. A previously saved diagram or unsupported package must have a visible way to search again for a playable version on the same revision. A failed/manual retry gives a local reason, and a temporary outage leaves the entry retryable. Reinstalling the app or first deleting a saved puzzle must never be a recovery step.
4. Keep distinct actions for confirmed native play, manual play on a saved image, viewing a reference, retrying discovery and reading the setter's post. Keep an offline worksheet and a future upgraded native attempt separate by edition. A manually claimed finish stays labeled as such. Offline use reads the local data and starts neither a resolver request nor an external app.

## Diagram-only puzzles

A square single-grid worksheet uses the setter's own saved image and rules. The player chooses the grid size and identifies opposite corners of the **printed** grid, reviews the aligned overlay, then enters marks on the grid. Keep outside clues in the original image. Persist configuration and entries per portal ID and edition in the Library. A wrong mark can be corrected. The player controls completion. Avoid interpreting an unusual picture as a standard Sudoku, fabricating a solution, or automatically revealing printed/fogged clues. Keep the picture available as a plain reference for puzzles unsuitable for a square overlay.

## Remaining native-format work for Phase 3

The live downloadable packages for *Killiar* (9 rows × 12 columns), *Word Search* (12×9) and *ɹɹ∀* (9×10) have nine 9-cell regions in the upper-left 9×9. Treat canvas bounds, playable cell mask, outside content and rule capabilities as separate properties. These editions need fixtures beyond an “opens” assertion. Keep *Word Search*'s exterior target words, alphabet and artwork; do not crop them off to make a square board. *Killiar* needs the setter's liar-cell rule and *ɹɹ∀* needs its halver/negator interpretation. Keep references accessible while unsupported. The Phase 3 format-coverage gate requires the full verified package and required art bytes retained for a later local decoder upgrade; a fingerprint by itself cannot meet that gate. Promote an edition to native play only once its geometry and all necessary information survive an offline round trip. Use manual checking when the full rule cannot be validated.
