# Phase 2 · Raising a creature and a civilization

**Player promise.** After the first amoeba, the player discovers what a creature becomes by choosing how it lives. Two creatures raised the same way share recognizable adaptations, but have their own colors, favorite digits, temperaments and relationships. The first nest, an occupied numeral and a torch passed from one neighbor to another should all be visible from the puzzle. This is the proposed expansion to the [original creator](creature-creator-specification.md) and [campaign](incremental-campaign-specification.md). The [Phase 2 delivery plan](phase-2-expansion.md) sets the acceptance gates.

## What Evolve actually does

Evolve opens on a cell accumulating RNA and DNA. Its early evolution menu presents currently available actions. On an ordinary fresh run, Sexual Reproduction opens **Phagocytosis**, **Chloroplasts** and **Chitin**, each costing 175 DNA. An achievement can add a later-run alternative, Exterminate. The choice advances the stage and closes the sibling actions for the run. After a shared Multicellular step, Bilateral Symmetry, Poikilohydric or Spores appears according to the earlier choice. Later branches can depend on both ancestry and the world, such as the mammal path on Eden unlocking Celestial. This gives the player an immediate consequence and a later discovery. At 100% progress, ordinary Sentience costs 300 RNA and 300 DNA, shows the genus emblem and draws a species from a defined list for that genus. Later-run conditions can expose named species buttons. For Sodukromental, adopt the early action menu, the conditional branches and the moment of discovery. Here the branches and individual seed assemble a body and its animations for **one creature**. A future hatch can explore another fork. Players discover names after hatching and can grow attached to an individual across prestige. ## A hatch from the player’s side

1. After clearing 5×5, open **Raise a helper**. Choose a lineage, such as **Plant**, then answer a question about its habitat. On the plant path, “Where does it wait out the wind?” offers **Brace beneath a floorboard** and **Follow the cracks**. The first youngster tests a load with two roots. The second sends a runner to the edge of the preview. Each answer labels its practical effect. One speeds the first material trip on a project and the other speeds the last.
2. An actual situation follows. “Two loose digits roll past the nest.” Choose **Catch both** or **Race after the first**. The first choice makes the plant cradle two copies. A mammal offered the same opportunity tucks them into a pouch. The consequence reads “Carries one extra copy of the requested digit.” There is no button for fur, legs, a finished race or a color.
3. **Back** previews the other life it could lead. **Review hatch** lists its lineage, decisions, demonstrated skill, price and free bed. **Hatch** commits the choices, rolls the individual once, reveals its name suggestion and lets the player name it. A returned draft retains its choices, sample silhouette and quote. A retry returns the same hatch. On narrow screens keep the growing silhouette, one question, two to four answers and Back/Continue in focus. At wide widths the preview can sit beside that same question.
4. Later, **Nurture** opens new situations for this individual between jobs. A confirmed founder, niche and expressed response become part of that creature’s life history. Nurture fills an empty eligible family. A new answer in an already filled family is a path for a later hatch. Draft choices remain freely reversible before commit. Acquired forms stay with the individual. Hatching again creates another individual. A recorded example in the field guide gives an environmental clue for a secret path. Once discovered, the guide remembers the choices that led there. A special form can be surprising even when its useful ability is explained in advance.

## Branches and the variety budget

Every founder offers two mutually exclusive niches. Each one produces a different rig, gait, and observable work effect. The names in parentheses below are for the design team and the discovery journal. The menu asks the questions in the middle column. Proposed times and prices are initial playtest values.

| Founder | Habitat alternatives offered to the player | Consequence of the first / second choice |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Plant | Brace beneath a floorboard / Follow the cracks | Root-walking trunk speeds the first glyph trip to a project / trailing runners speed its final outstanding glyph trip. |
| Mammalian | Hunt along open trails / Shelter what you find | Fur and a four-footed gait speed a favorite-digit request / upright pouch carrier speeds a manually prioritized digit trip. |
| Arthropod | Gather from a heap / Span the gap with silk | Six-legged pincer rig speeds a multi-copy order / a silk-spinning rig speeds collection of marked sentence letters. |
| Reptilian | Follow a tipping ledge / Shelter beside the work site | Low, articulated skink speeds digit trips during tilt / shell-backed carrier speeds urgent work outside its preferred assignment. |
| Fungal | Fruit in lantern light / Follow a buried thread | A walking cap speeds starter-torch travel after the lesson / corded mycelium speeds a multi-anchor reveal. |
| Gel colony | Follow a changing current / Divide across two trails | A rippling body speeds its first trip after changing work type / two connected lobes speed a trip when two eligible jobs are waiting at dispatch. |

For each situation require an inherited physical expression as well as a work effect. A pair-catcher grows paired cargo sockets, a roof-fitter develops a bracing joint, a starter scout grows a forward lamp mount and a relay worker develops a handoff pose. Each of the four responses in a family must have a distinct rig part, movement signature or tool interaction on every niche. Crossing families composes attachments. A root-walking scout with two cargo sockets can lean its lamp forward while both loads sway. A shell-backed scout anchors the lamp to a moving rim instead. Store the resulting mounts and test the whole combination in motion.

Each niche bonus multiplies a qualifying trip by 0.88. The six roots are fantasy developmental families. All six are available at the first designed hatch. Environment, earlier choices and game milestones determine subsequent situations. For each of the twelve niches the content manifest must supply lineage-specific verbs, limb mounts and poses for the following four decisions. At full progression every combination in the table is reachable. A branch may change the verbs and the way an ability is performed without removing an ability from the count.

| Situation family | Four distinct actions and their effects on work |
| --- | --- |
| A digit gets loose | Chase it, digit travel ×0.75 · Gather a pair, +1 same-digit cargo · Brace against a slope, tilt digit travel ×0.65 · Keep a favorite nearby, +1 selected favorite restock target. |
| The colony needs a roof | Collect in bundles, +1 same-project glyph · Hurry every load, glyph travel ×0.80 · Fit the supports, support travel ×0.70 · Read the settlement notice, notice-glyph travel ×0.70. |
| A path goes dark | Hurry the flame, torch travel ×0.75 · Follow a linked signal, Linked travel ×0.65 · Scout the entrance, post-tutorial starter travel ×0.65 · Carry two earned lights, batch at most two body-compatible legs on already earned independent patches. Each distinct arrival/guard still needs its own eligible worker when the encounter calls for one. |
| Neighbors need to coordinate | Inspect a visible conflict on request · Hold an offer two seconds longer · Relay a load, next same-project handoff to a teammate ×0.85 · Cover an urgent assignment outside preference, travel ×0.85. |

Unlock **Chase/Gather** at 5×5 and the other two digit responses after 6×6. The first two roof responses open after 7×7. The city opens the rest and introduces coordination. Halo opens light responses, including scouting for later boards. The final two coordination responses and fourth active decision open after Arrows. An individual has one active decision in each earned family, with a total cap of one at 5×5, two after the 7×7 clear, three at Halo and four at civilization. At 7×7 the player can immediately nurture its first roof response. It can also leave that slot open for a later situation. Earlier decisions appear as context or prerequisites for discoveries. Round travel once after multiplying qualifying effects, with a 0.50 base-duration floor. Favorite stock applies once per value. Show which bonus fired in job details.

**3,072 ordinary adult builds** is the acceptance floor. The calculation is 6 founders × 2 niches × 4 digit responses × 4 roof responses × 4 light responses × 4 coordination responses. It excludes color, favorite, personality, limb-count seeds, click order, unfinished builds and secrets. Enumerate the legal graph from a fresh run through Arrows, canonicalize resolved choices, and require 3,072 reachable signatures. Exercise qualifying trip, capacity, stock, reveal and cooperation fixtures for each decision, and compare rigs at sprite scale. Four buttons that converge on the same effects and poses cannot support four counted outcomes. The first hatch need only ask three questions.

## Discoveries and individual identity

These are worked discovery recipes, not species buttons. The ancestry and choices grow a skeleton procedurally. All four examples retain the individual's own silhouette details, joints, colors, disposition and favorite. Discoveries are optional and excluded from the variety floor.

| What the player does | What can emerge and what the player sees |
| --- | --- |
| Raise a sheltering mammal, gather pairs, fit supports and relay a load after seeing a neighbor finish a beam | An upright, humanlike tool user frees its forelimbs to use a lever and passes the beam hand to hand. |
| Raise a bracing plant, fit supports and cover an urgent assignment after witnessing a roof go up | An ent-like walking tree knots a roof brace into its crown. Its root gait plants one foot while a branch bears the load. |
| Raise a fruiting fungus, scout the entrance and relay a load after witnessing a real starter torch delivery | A lantern-cap grows lit gills. The shade under the cap carries the flame during the handoff. |
| Raise a divided gel colony, brace on a slope, collect bundles and carry two earned lights after finding a doubled footprint in Trail fog | The two lobes disagree about their front end. A mouth in the knee yawns while an extra hand passes a lamp backwards. This unsettling individual can still build, socialize, be named and be kept. |

The journal records the witnessed event ID and a plain-language clue. Once the choices and event match, offer a contextual action, such as **Follow the second footprint**, preview the unusual gait and keep the ordinary path available. A secret can change a role's performance animation while using its existing numerical effect. Evaluate rewards and ordinary build signatures independently of the discovery. Each secret must have a repeatable in-game observation route. Place demo hints on re-enterable encounters or replay an already witnessed event from the journal.

Use a versioned path graph with stable node IDs, explicit prerequisites and an effect/rig projection. Store chosen ancestry, path, hatch command and resolved phenotype. Give paint, markings, appendage count, favorite digit, personality and relationships independent random streams. Sample the favorite uniformly over digits available at birth, except the first amoeba always favors 4. Validate mounts and joint ranges for asymmetric, legless and many-legged creatures. Walk, run, crouch, carry, poke, recoil, plant, tussle and return poses must keep feet, grip and cargo attached. For the 5×5 first hatch, keep the 4 DNA / 8 RNA price. Designed hatch K costs `ceil(4 × 1.45^(K−1))` DNA and `ceil(8 × 1.45^(K−1))` RNA, plus 1 DNA / 2 RNA per additional active response after the first. Nurturing pays that increment only for each newly filled family. No charge for preview or an interrupted confirmation. Legacy paid abilities retain their identity and terms on import.

## What happens around the board

| Trigger | Visible interaction | Mechanical consequence and player control |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A favorite digit is in stock or a tile has just been verified | Interested neighbors flock, lift or point. One shrinks into the margin of a verified placed numeral and peeks out again. | Offer **Move in** on a verified numeral. A resident marks it as its address for this board, sleeps there between trips and steps out to work. Persist that address on reload. A tile can host one resident. Its ID, value and hit target remain intact. A resident can hold one stock reservation or stand watch, never both. Undo, moving the tile or changing boards evicts it gently to its home. In a manual-check puzzle there is no correctness signal to leak. |
| A visible contradiction appears | A curious helper tilts its head, a hothead stomps or pounds a loose wrong tile, and a collector tries to carry it home. | Use the existing visible-conflict and recovery rules. **Bring back** returns the exact piece or the existing salvage replacement entitlement once. A repeated invalid move produces no currency or building stock. |
| A flavor sentence has a marked glyph | A curious creature pokes it. A mischievous neighbor pockets it, another objects, and it carries the glyph back or delivers it to a requested project. | Reserve by glyph ID. **Return letter** interrupts the prank. Return automatically within four active seconds if no authorized project owns it. Only authored material copies can move. Puzzle rules, tutor words and unspent tutorial 4s stay readable and operable. |
| Two neighbors meet | Friendly pairs greet and hand over a beam. A rival pair argues, then may have a short slapstick scuffle and separate. They remember the meeting. | Each individual has stable sociability, curiosity, tidiness, patience and boldness. Use a stable draw from the ordered pair of IDs, add a bonus for shared favorites or complementary work habits, and add bounded memory of cooperative trips and disputes. Store one relationship per pair with a score from −3 to +3. A score of +2 favors greetings or handoffs. −2 permits arguments and a −3 pair can tussle. Limit the pair to one dispute per 60 active seconds. A jointly completed job can improve the score. Friends can earn a handoff bonus. Rivals take separate lanes or request reassignment. A scuffle lasts up to two seconds, preempts on player work, never destroys a creature or its cargo. Provide **Work apart** and a quiet/reduced-antics option. |
| A project accepts a letter | The actual collector walks to the glyph, crouches, lifts it, carries it to the foundation and fits it into a marked socket. A neighbor can steady the beam. | Show committed sockets separately from reserved/in-transit pieces. Reopening resumes the actual load. The final occupied socket completes the project once. Construction is visible on the play screen. |

Run the scene from authoritative actor, job, cargo, project and destination IDs. A scenic sprite cannot fulfill a staffing requirement. Show at most six foreground actors on a small screen, prioritizing jobs and bonds. The population list includes every resident. Let other residents visit in rotation. Use the existing Hum palette, articulated pixel rigs and paper board. On a narrow phone the compact habitat below the number pad shows the half-built silhouette, its most recent worker and **Nest · 2 of 4 pieces**. At wide widths show a side street with source, homes and work sites. Actors can visit the board margins and verified numerals. Their art passes through input, and the same actions are available from the focusable resident/project list. Reduced motion replaces trips and fights with endpoints and short text, preserving arrival time and work outcome.

### How creatures move across the play screen

The habitat is a home base. In ordinary campaign play, the same named creature can leave its doorway, travel along the number pad, approach the puzzle text and return to its building. Use the visible doorway, pad slot, harvestable glyph, edge of an eligible cell and construction socket as destinations. Recompute their screen positions when the player scrolls or resizes the board. If a destination is offscreen, let the worker leave or enter at the corresponding edge and complete the same job on schedule. Choose destinations using only information visible to the player. Keep digit faces, pencil marks, fog clues, controls and the player’s drag path clear. Creature art passes touch through to the board. Its offer or recall action remains available from the pad or resident list.

| Moment | What should be visible at gameplay size |
| --- | --- |
| Between jobs | A free resident looks toward a visible favorite, turns and walks over to watch it. Another may stop to poke a marked letter, greet a neighbor or curl up in its numeral home. Give each a destination, a pause and a reason to move again. Personality changes the approach and reaction. An urgent assignment can interrupt the visit. |
| Walking, running and hauling | Drive each generated rig’s stride by the distance traveled. A two-legged carrier plants alternate feet. A four-legged runner gathers its feet under the body. A many-legged rig phases its available limbs in groups. A legless gel reaches forward, transfers its weight and draws its rear edge after it. Carrying takes over the relevant grip or pseudopod while the remaining limbs keep moving. Preserve the individual’s silhouette, colors and markings through turns and speed changes. |
| A job the player can follow | A builder leaves the nest, walks to a marked letter in the description, braces, pulls it free and carries that exact glyph back. It turns at the board edge, fits the piece into the roof and lets go as the socket fills. A paired digit carrier visibly supports both copies. A torchbearer raises its flame at the earned patch. Use these contacts to show which worker did the work and when ownership changed. |

Work animation follows the job clock. Pickup, travel and handoff fit inside the quoted trip time. A frame drop, skipped animation or offscreen leg cannot advance a reveal, duplicate cargo or extend the wait. After resume, show the named carrier on the current leg or show the completed delivery. In reduced motion, use stationary departure and arrival poses, visible cargo and a short job status. When an eligible retained companion visits the app’s Home or Library screen, use its familiar gait and that screen’s safe waypoints. Recall it when its colony job takes priority. Android outings follow the existing opt-in. [Companion visits](companion-specification.md#learning-and-roaming)

**Motion evidence for the debug handoff.** Record a normal-speed Android play session at phone size. In a seeded 20-second idle scene with an awake, available resident and normal motion, show it travel between two distinct landmarks while the player can still select a cell. Then follow one named builder from the text to a roof socket in a single shot. Review walking, running and loaded turns on two-, four-, many-legged and legless test individuals. Check that a planted foot holds its position through a step and that a gel’s leading and trailing edges move in sequence. At pickup and release, verify the grip meets the item and the installed piece corresponds to the one carried. Repeat a delivery with six foreground residents while dragging a digit, then with reduced motion and a mid-trip resume. The destination, credited worker, tile or glyph count and job deadline must agree across runs.

## From a nest to a civilization

These stages extend the established clear milestones. Population is an owned roster with stable IDs, names, homes, roles and memories. The player can bond with any resident. Designed births buy tailored abilities. Residents born or arriving through the settlement start with one lineage-appropriate basic role and can learn. Work an assigned resident performs counts exactly as work performed by a manually raised creature.

| Stage and trigger | Visible construction and population | Decision and benefit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Nest, after 5×5 | Founders gather one roof, two supports and one ring from marked text. Start with two personal berths. The nest adds a third. | A second helper fetches digits while the first builds. Sprites place collected pieces in sockets in view of the current puzzle. |
| Village, after 6×6 | Open a common house with 4 communal places. Residents populate it autonomously. Build homes and a storehouse from letters. | Up to 2 field posts. Staff a restock value or a building site. A helper can learn a trade at the common house, then demonstrate it on its next job. |
| City, after 8×8 | Two neighborhoods have a combined 8 communal places. Builders lay a street between houses, the workshop and a lamp station. | Up to 3 field posts. Allocate a crew between digit requests, building and, after Halo, torches. Prioritize lamps to see sooner or leave a carrier on the pad. Moving one post shows both changed arrival estimates. |
| Civilization, after Arrows | A district of up to 12 communal residents has a council and a Meeting Hall project. Two roof pieces, four supports and two beams visibly become the hall. | Up to 4 field posts. Shared Reserves covers two extra selected stock values. Shared Crews lends idle hands across districts to pending builds. Independent is the opt-out for players who want local assignments with neither shared behavior. Choose one active policy at a time. Display a glyph or lamp associated with a named contributor. A completed hall and a route finale open prestige. |

Communal caps are additional to personal beds. Model each recruited resident as one body. On founding a village, grant one arrival credit. Each later first-time campaign clear grants one. Each 60 seconds of elapsed settlement time also accrues one arrival credit, using the existing two-minute offline cap. Cap banked credits at two. By default, spend one available credit on one new resident when a communal bed is free. A paused settlement can **Welcome resident** manually. A sole founder can welcome a newcomer. Two residents can raise young. A new arrival never requires an eligible pair or a paid hatch. Automatic residents have their own saved birth sequence and do not raise the price of a designed hatch. Use one transaction per arrival so relaunching cannot duplicate a child. Residents gain no automatic puzzle-solving authority. New staffed jobs use real available workers up to the stage post limit. Other residents build relationships and take turns at the next dispatch.

The population clock stores last accounted time, remaining seconds and grant IDs keyed by run and first clear. Pausing arrivals pauses spending. Credits can still accumulate up to two. Discard surplus elapsed intervals at the cap so reopening housing cannot create a backlog. Charge or spend a credit, mint a stable resident ID and assign its berth in one transaction. Every worker, including an untrained recruit, can do the basic digit, glyph and lamp jobs. An unlocked trade improves a trip. Under One Helper, automatic residents can live and socialize, but every working role, including shattering, building and lamp carrying, belongs to the original amoeba.

**Projects that change daily play.** At 7×7 a House uses 1 roof, 3 supports, 1 ring and 1 beam for two more personal beds. A Storehouse uses 1 roof, 2 supports and 2 beams. Stage selected stock there so the player can see missing inventory. At the city, a Workshop uses 1 roof, 3 supports and 2 beams. Use it to queue one resident’s trade lesson for a family the city has unlocked. Reserve the normal nurture cost when the player approves the lesson. Spend it once on completion or release it on cancellation. Show the job ETA before and after training. One district may prioritize restock while another installs the next beam. After completing the hall, let the civilization choose a lit route marker or a letter-built memorial, supply four ordinary material copies (1 roof, 2 supports, 1 beam), and choose a contributing resident to credit. Its persistent site and journal entry record the run’s choice. This is an optional project and awards no extra prestige currency.

**Building ownership.** Each socket accepts one typed glyph ID. The player starts or pauses a project and may allocate shared stock to it. A collector reserves the glyph and socket, walks the letter to the site, and commits that socket once on arrival. Canceling releases an in-transit reservation once. Installed pieces stay in their sockets. The last socket completes the project and unlocks its benefit atomically. On upgrade, an old completed structure retains its installed glyphs. Unallocated stock, held letters and existing deliveries retain their single owner. The settlement notice issues new, uniquely identified typed material sufficient for any planned building, so a public work cannot starve the Meeting Hall.

The village can populate itself even when the player spends every resource on producers. For example, an amoeba arriving alone at village founding gains a second resident with its founding credit. A subsequent first clear or one minute supplies a third. At civilization four residents fit within the communal cap. The staffed fog gates in the [board specification](phase-2-expansion.md#owned-torches) therefore have a finite recovery path. A deliberate **Raise a helper** spends DNA/RNA to choose a specialist immediately when a personal bed is free.

Apply the [finite producer batches and settlement orders](phase-2-incremental-balance.md) to these jobs. Keep the ordinary first-clear rewards. Optional benefits compete for worker time rather than paying DNA per tap or argument. A second worker should measurably reduce the time for two independent required jobs. Two bonded workers should visibly fit the same beam faster when a qualifying trait applies. Report these as measured scenarios in the next playtest. Save the patron's name, the chosen companion's discoveries and the completed hall in the archive on prestige. Reset settlement jobs, buildings, population and run currency once. The retained creature keeps its individual traits and learned memories. Fresh relationships use current resident IDs, while archived bonds stay in its history. Preserve the existing charter rules and earned Legacy credits.
