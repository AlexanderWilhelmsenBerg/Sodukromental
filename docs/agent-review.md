Record the slice, observed behavior, reproduction, owner response and independent retest evidence. For a spec review, identify the resulting change and leave the runtime result pending until exercised.

Mechanics added missing-version compatibility and boundary validation. | `assurance_boards_test.dart` passed current and unversioned records and rejected future versions and invalid rule and fog cells. |
| C5 | Initial content exposed almost the full board after the starter torch. The coordinator narrowed the starter patches and authored later reveals. | Visible-only completion and trigger coverage passed on the revised assets. |

A parent rebuild could reset the cursor to a prior tap selection.
